Deal Governance · B2B Sales · Pricing Operations · Digital Platform

Structured Deal Governance Framework — RAK Ceramics India

This study examines the design and implementation of a structured deal governance framework for RAK Ceramics India's project-based sales channel, operating across DC 31 (dealer-led) and DC 32 (direct developer) distribution codes. The research addresses a critical operational gap in high-value B2B construction material sales, where unstructured discount approvals, inconsistent margin computation, and absent SLA enforcement create commercial leakage and governance risk.

The framework formalises a three-tier approval authority matrix — Deal Desk, PM Head, and CEO — calibrated against Net Sales Realization-based margin thresholds distinct to two product lines: Tiles and Bathware. It operationalises the pricing waterfall from List Price through Overriding Commission deduction to NSR, enforcing five governing rules on ORC computation, NEF derivation, and margin basis discipline. SLA targets of 6, 24, and 48 business hours are assigned by approval level, with structured escalation triggers, quote versioning controls, and a 30-day PO receipt governance cycle.

The framework additionally surfaces competitive intelligence captured at the deal level, enabling state-wise pricing analysis and dealer performance evaluation through structured scorecards. A web-based operational platform was developed to digitise and enforce this framework in real time, replacing manual email-based workflows with auditable, Firestore-backed deal tracking and approval management.

OUTCOME: Three-tier approval authority formalised across DC 31 & DC 32. SLA enforcement (6 / 24 / 48 hrs) with structured escalation. Firestore-backed deal tracking platform replacing manual email workflows — eliminating commercial leakage and enabling state-wise competitive intelligence.
