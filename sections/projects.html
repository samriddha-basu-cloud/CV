Deal Governance · B2B Commercial Operations · Pricing Architecture · Digital Platform

Deal Governance & Pricing Control Framework — RAK Ceramics India

RAK Ceramics India's project-based sales channel — spanning DC 31 (dealer-led) and DC 32 (direct developer) distribution codes — lacked a formalised commercial control structure. Discount approvals were unstructured, margin computation was inconsistent, and SLA enforcement was absent across deal cycles. The result was measurable commercial leakage and a governance gap in high-value B2B construction material transactions.

I designed and implemented a structured deal governance framework that formalised a three-tier approval authority matrix — Deal Desk, PM Head, and CEO — calibrated against Net Sales Realization-based margin thresholds specific to Tiles and Bathware product lines. The framework operationalised the full pricing waterfall from List Price through Overriding Commission deduction to NSR, enforcing five governing rules on ORC computation, NEF derivation, and margin basis discipline.

SLA targets of 6, 24, and 48 business hours were assigned by approval tier, supported by structured escalation triggers, quote versioning controls, and a 30-day PO receipt governance cycle. At the deal level, the framework captures competitive intelligence — enabling state-wise pricing analysis and dealer performance evaluation through structured scorecards. A Firestore-backed web platform enforces the framework in real time, fully replacing manual email approval chains with an auditable, structured workflow.
